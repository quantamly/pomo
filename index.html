Why Nari works

Focus in sprints.
One bounded block of attention, then a real break. Short, finite effort beats endless willpower — and breaks keep your mind fresh.
Let the clock surprise you.
Fixed timers make you clock-watch. Nari draws each focus and break at random from your range, so you just work until the bell.
Calm on purpose.
Soft nature scenes and gentle chimes lower arousal — easier to drop in, easier to step away without a jolt.
Tiny rewards.
Fresh lines and little pop-ups add unpredictable delight, so returning to focus feels good, not grim.
